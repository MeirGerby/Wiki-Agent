# Step 1 — Assess

Goal: find the learner's starting point before explaining anything.

1. Read `learn/profile.md` (known concepts, gaps, preferred style). Skip questions it already answers.
2. Find the target topic's pages in the wiki and list the concepts it depends on.
3. Infer what you can from the learner's wording: terms they use correctly, terms they avoid, mistakes.
4. Ask up to 3 short questions about the gaps that remain, for example:
   - "Have you used X before? What did it do for you?"
   - "How would you explain Y in one sentence?"
   - "What are you trying to do with this?"
5. Write a one-line level estimate: `novice | partial | working | strong`, plus the missing prerequisites.

Output: a short summary of the estimate. Do not teach yet.
