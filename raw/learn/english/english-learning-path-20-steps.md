# English Learning Guide in 20 Steps 🇬🇧

Goal: progress step by step from basic English to holding a conversation, understanding videos and reading texts in English.

(English translation of the Hebrew guide supplied by the user. Step-by-step lesson files are in `steps/`.)

## Stage A — Language foundations

### 1. Level assessment

Test your level in reading, listening, speaking and grammar. This shows you where to start.

### 2. Basic vocabulary

Learn 10 words a day from everyday topics: home, work, food, time and transport.

### 3. Sentence structure

Learn the basic order: subject + verb + object.

Example: `I learn English.`

### 4. The verb "to be"

Practice `am`, `is`, `are` in positive sentences, negative sentences and questions.

### 5. Basic tenses

Learn Present Simple first, then Present Continuous, and finally Past Simple.

## Stage B — Understanding English

### 6. Daily listening

Listen to simple English for 5-10 minutes. Try to understand the general idea before translating.

### 7. Pronunciation

Listen to a word, repeat it aloud, and compare it with the original pronunciation.

### 8. Graded reading

Read short texts that suit your level. Mark words that keep coming back.

### 9. Whole phrases

Learn `How are you?` and `I would like...` instead of memorizing single words only.

### 10. Listening comprehension with subtitles

Watch a short video with English subtitles, listen again without subtitles, and check what improved.

## Stage C — Moving to active learning

### 11. Building questions

Practice `Do you...?`, `Did you...?`, `Where...?` and `Why...?`.

### 12. Negation

Learn the difference between `don't`, `doesn't`, `didn't` and `isn't`.

### 13. Talking to yourself

Describe your actions aloud in English: `I am making coffee.`

### 14. Daily writing

Write 5 sentences about your day. Check for mistakes and rewrite the sentences correctly.

### 15. Practical conversation

Practice a 5-minute conversation: introductions, ordering food, asking for help, or a work conversation.

## Stage D — Intermediate level and above

### 16. Advanced tenses

Learn Future, Present Perfect and Past Continuous as needed.

### 17. Linking words

Practice `although`, `however`, `therefore`, `because` and `unless`.

### 18. Professional English

Learn terms from your field, for example Linux, programming and cyber, and write sentences with them.

### 19. Thinking in English

Try to phrase simple thoughts directly in English, without translating every sentence from Hebrew.

### 20. Progress test

Record yourself speaking for 3 minutes, read a new text and summarize it in English. Compare with earlier results and plan next week according to your weak points.

## Recommended daily study routine

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 10 minutes | Review words |
| 10 minutes | Grammar and sentences |
| 10 minutes | Listening |
| 10 minutes | Speaking or writing |

The key principle: study a little every day, use the material actively, and review at spaced intervals. Do not settle for reading explanations.
