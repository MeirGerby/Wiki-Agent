# Step 11 — Building Questions

Stage C (Active learning). Goal: ask questions with `Do you...?`, `Did you...?`, `Where...?` and `Why...?`.

## Learn

- Yes/No questions use an auxiliary first: `Do you like coffee?` `Does she work here?` `Did they call?`
- The main verb stays in the base form after `do/does/did`: `Did you go?` (not `Did you went?`).
- Question words come first: `What, Where, When, Why, Who, How, How much/many`.
- Order for wh-questions: **question word + auxiliary + subject + verb**: `Where do you live?` `Why did he leave?`
- With `to be`, swap the order: `Is she here?` `Where are you?`

## Guided exercise

1. Turn statements into Yes/No questions: `You like tea.` `She works late.` `They visited Rome.`
2. Make wh-questions from a statement, one for each part: `Dan bought a car in March.` -> `Who bought a car? What did Dan buy? When did Dan buy a car?`
3. Interview practice: write 8 questions to ask a new colleague (name, job, city, hobbies, plans), then answer them yourself aloud.
4. Fix: `Where you live?` `Did you went?` `What means this?`
5. Speed drill: say the question for 10 answers read aloud by you: `At 7.` -> `What time do you wake up?`

## What good looks like

- You choose `do / does / did` automatically.
- You keep the verb in its base form after the auxiliary.

## Common mistakes for Hebrew speakers

- Making a question by raising the voice only: `You live here?`
- Leaving out the auxiliary: `Where you live?`
- Double marking the past: `Did you went?`
- `What means...?` -> `What does ... mean?`

## Daily summary prompts

Write 10 questions (5 yes/no, 5 wh-) about a person you know.

## Review questions

1. What comes after `Did` in a question: `go` or `went`?
2. Give the word order of a wh-question.
3. How do you ask about the meaning of a word?
