# Step 7 — Pronunciation

Stage B. Goal: hear a word, repeat it aloud, and compare it with the original.

## Learn

- Method: **listen, repeat aloud, record, compare**. Doing it silently does not count.
- Word stress: English words have one strong syllable (`PHOtograph`, `phoTOgrapher`, `photoGRAPHic`). Wrong stress hurts understanding more than a wrong letter sound.
- Sentence rhythm: important words (nouns, verbs, adjectives) are stronger; small words (`to`, `of`, `the`) are short and weak.
- Use a dictionary with audio and the IPA symbol, and shadowing (repeat right after a speaker).

## Guided exercise (10 minutes)

1. Pick 5 words from today's vocabulary. Listen to each one in an online dictionary with audio.
2. Say each word 3 times aloud.
3. Record yourself saying all 5. Play the original and your recording one after the other. Note one difference per word.
4. Shadowing: take one 10-second sentence from a clip. Repeat it right after the speaker 5 times, matching rhythm and speed.
5. Work on one sound pair per day (see below), with minimal pairs: `ship / sheep`, `think / sink`, `very / berry`, `light / right`.

## Sounds that need attention for Hebrew speakers

| Sound | Example | Tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| /θ/ and /ð/ | `think`, `this` | Tongue tip lightly between the teeth |
| /w/ vs /v/ | `west` / `vest` | /w/ with rounded lips, no teeth on lip |
| /r/ | `red`, `right` | Tongue does not touch the mouth; not the Hebrew guttural resh |
| Short vs long vowels | `ship` / `sheep` | Hold the long vowel longer |
| Final consonants | `bed`, `bag` | Say the last sound; do not drop it |

## What good looks like

- Your recording sounds close to the original in stress and rhythm.
- You can hear the difference in minimal pairs.

## Common mistakes

- Pronouncing the word as it is spelled (`comfortable`, `Wednesday`). Check the audio.
- Putting the stress on the wrong syllable.
- Speaking every word with the same strength.

## Daily summary prompts

Write the 5 words with IPA or a stress mark, and one difference you fixed.

## Review questions

1. What is word stress?
2. How do you form /θ/?
3. Why record yourself?
