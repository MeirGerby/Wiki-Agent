# Step 14 — Daily Writing

Stage C. Goal: write 5 sentences about your day, check for mistakes and rewrite them correctly.

## Learn

- Writing is slower than speaking, so you can notice and fix mistakes.
- Keep a **mistake log**. Repeated mistakes are your real syllabus.
- Write simple sentences first; join them later with `and`, `but`, `because` (step 17).
- Read your text aloud once. Your ear catches what your eye misses.

## Guided exercise (10 minutes)

1. Write 5 sentences about today. Use at least one new word and one new phrase.
   ```
   Today I woke up at 7. I drank coffee and read the news. ...
   ```
2. **Check**: for each sentence ask: Subject? Verb? Right tense? Singular/plural? `-s`? Articles `a/the`? Prepositions?
3. **Correct**: rewrite every wrong sentence. Do not delete it; keep the original and the fix.
4. Log the mistake type:

```markdown
Date: ...
Original: She go to work.
Fixed:    She goes to work.
Rule: -s with he/she/it
```

5. At the end of the week, count mistake types. Pick the top one for next week's focus.
6. Optional: ask someone, or a tool, to check one text a week, and compare with your own corrections.

## What good looks like

- Each day ends with 5 corrected sentences.
- The same mistake appears less often over the weeks.

## Common mistakes for Hebrew speakers

- Missing articles (`a`, `the`): Hebrew has only a definite article.
- Wrong prepositions: `depend of` -> `depend on`, `married with` -> `married to`.
- Missing plural `-s`: `two book` -> `two books`.
- Writing long sentences copied from Hebrew structure.

## Daily summary prompts

Paste today's 5 corrected sentences and the one rule you learned.

## Review questions

1. Why keep a mistake log?
2. What three checks catch most beginner errors?
3. Why read the text aloud?
