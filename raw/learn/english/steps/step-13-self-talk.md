# Step 13 — Talking to Yourself

Stage C. Goal: describe your actions aloud in English, for example `I am making coffee.`

## Learn

- Narrating what you do builds fluency without needing a partner.
- Use Present Continuous for what you do now: `I'm making coffee. I'm opening the fridge.`
- Use Past Simple for what just happened: `I woke up late. I made breakfast.`
- If you do not know a word, use a simpler one or describe it (`the thing for cutting` -> `knife`). Write the missing word down for later.

## Guided exercise (10 minutes)

1. **Live narration** (3 min): while you cook, tidy or walk, describe each action aloud in full sentences.
2. **Missing words**: keep a note for every word you could not say. Look them up after, and add them to your word list.
3. **Replay** (3 min): after the activity, tell what you did using Past Simple.
4. **Plan** (2 min): tell what you will do next, with `I'm going to...`.
5. **Mirror question**: ask yourself 3 questions and answer them: `What did I do today? Why am I tired? What am I going to eat?`
6. Record one session per week to compare in step 20.

## What good looks like

- You keep speaking even when you do not know a word.
- Your list of missing words turns into new vocabulary.

## Common mistakes for Hebrew speakers

- Stopping to translate in your head instead of using simple words.
- Using Present Simple for actions in progress: `I make coffee now` -> `I'm making coffee`.
- Whispering. Speak aloud, even quietly, so the mouth practises.

## Daily summary prompts

Write 5 of the sentences you said, and the words you were missing.

## Review questions

1. Which tense do you use for what is happening now?
2. What do you do when you do not know a word?
3. Why speak aloud and not silently?
