# Step 18 — Professional English

Stage D. Goal: learn terms from your field, for example Linux, programming and cyber, and write sentences with them.

## Learn

- Your field has its own vocabulary: verbs (`deploy, restart, configure`), nouns (`log, server, permission`), and phrases (`the service is down`).
- Learn terms in context from real documentation: man pages, README files, error messages, the wiki in this repo.
- Many terms are also verbs and nouns: `a commit` / `to commit`, `a log` / `to log`.
- Learn how to explain a command in plain English: `This command lists all open ports.`

## Guided exercise

1. Pick 10 terms from your current study (from the Linux cyber path, for example): `permission, process, service, firewall, log, packet, port, key, hash, evidence`.
2. For each write: meaning, an example sentence, pronunciation check.
   - `A firewall blocks traffic that is not allowed.`
3. **Explain a command**: write two sentences for each: `ss -tulpn` -> `This command shows the ports that are listening and the programs that use them.`
4. **Read an error message** aloud and say it in your own words: `Permission denied` -> `The user is not allowed to open this file.`
5. **Write a short report** (5-6 lines) in English: `I checked the failed logins. One IP address tried 12 times. I blocked it and changed the password.`
6. Say a 1-minute explanation of one concept (for example, what a firewall does) to a friend or to your recorder.

## What good looks like

- You can describe what you did in a lab, in English, in plain words.
- You use the right verb with each term (`restart a service`, `block an address`, `open a port`).

## Common mistakes for Hebrew speakers

- Mixing Hebrew and English in the same sentence out of habit.
- Wrong prepositions: `connect to`, `log in to`, `depend on`.
- Plural forms: `data` is usually singular in tech speech; `a log`, `two logs`.

## Daily summary prompts

Write the 10 terms with one sentence each, and one short report about today's technical work.

## Review questions

1. How do you say in English what `ss -tulpn` does?
2. Which verbs go with `service` and `firewall`?
3. Why learn terms from real documentation?
