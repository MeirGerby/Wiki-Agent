# Step 5 — Basic Tenses

Stage A. Goal: use Present Simple, Present Continuous and Past Simple, learned in this order.

## Learn

| Tense | Use | Form | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Present Simple | habits, facts, routines | verb (+ s for he/she/it) | `She works in Tel Aviv.` |
| Present Continuous | happening now, temporary | am/is/are + verb-ing | `I am reading now.` |
| Past Simple | finished action in the past | verb-ed or irregular form | `We visited Haifa. I saw a movie.` |

- Time words: Present Simple (`always, usually, every day`), Continuous (`now, at the moment, today`), Past (`yesterday, last week, in 2020`).
- Learn the top 20 irregular past verbs first: `go-went, have-had, do-did, see-saw, make-made, take-took, come-came, get-got, eat-ate, buy-bought`.

## Guided exercise (one tense per few days)

1. **Present Simple**: write 5 sentences about your routine; add the `-s` for `he/she`. `He wakes up at 7.`
2. **Present Continuous**: look around and describe 5 things happening right now. `My neighbor is talking on the phone.`
3. **Past Simple**: write 5 sentences about yesterday with a time word.
4. Take one action and write it in all three tenses: `I drink coffee every day. I am drinking coffee now. I drank coffee yesterday.`
5. Sort 10 mixed sentences by tense and fix the wrong ones.

## What good looks like

- You pick the tense from the time word.
- You know the irregular verbs without a list.

## Common mistakes for Hebrew speakers

- Forgetting the `-s`: `She work` -> `She works`.
- Using Present Simple for "now": `I read now` -> `I am reading now`.
- Regularizing irregular verbs: `goed`, `buyed` -> `went`, `bought`.
- Missing `did` in questions/negatives is covered in steps 11-12.

## Daily summary prompts

Write the same event in the three tenses with a time word for each.

## Review questions

1. Which tense goes with `every day`? with `now`? with `yesterday`?
2. What is the past of `go`, `eat`, `buy`?
3. When do you add `-s` to a verb?
