# Step 17 — Linking Words

Stage D. Goal: practise `although`, `however`, `therefore`, `because` and `unless`.

## Learn

| Word | Meaning | Position and punctuation | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| because | reason | joins two clauses | `I stayed home because I was tired.` |
| therefore | result | starts a new clause, with a comma | `I was tired. Therefore, I stayed home.` |
| although | contrast | joins two clauses | `Although I was tired, I went out.` |
| however | contrast | starts a new sentence, with a comma | `I was tired. However, I went out.` |
| unless | condition ("if not") | joins two clauses | `I'll go unless it rains.` |

- `because` + clause; `because of` + noun: `because of the rain`.
- `although` and `however` mean the same idea but are used differently. Do not put `but` and `although` in the same sentence.

## Guided exercise

1. Join each pair with the word in brackets:
   - `I like English. It is difficult.` (although)
   - `It was cold. We stayed inside.` (therefore)
   - `We will be late. We hurry.` (unless)
2. Rewrite one sentence three ways: `He worked hard, but he failed.` -> `Although he worked hard, he failed.` / `He worked hard. However, he failed.`
3. Write 2 sentences of your own for each word, about your studies or work.
4. Add linking words to your daily writing (step 14): at least two per day.
5. Spot the error: `Although he is rich, but he is not happy.` `Because I was tired, therefore I left.`

## What good looks like

- Your sentences are connected instead of a list of short ones.
- You punctuate `however` and `therefore` correctly.

## Common mistakes for Hebrew speakers

- Using two connectors for one idea: `Although..., but...` or `Because..., so...`.
- Missing the comma after `however` and `therefore`.
- Using `unless` with a negative verb: `unless you don't go` -> `unless you go`.

## Daily summary prompts

Write a short paragraph (4-5 sentences) using at least three of the five words.

## Review questions

1. What is the difference between `although` and `however`?
2. Why is `unless it doesn't rain` wrong?
3. What follows `because of`?
