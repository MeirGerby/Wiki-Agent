# Step 3 — Sentence Structure

Stage A. Goal: build correct basic sentences with the order subject + verb + object.

## Learn

- The basic order: **S**ubject + **V**erb + **O**bject. Example: `I learn English.`
- Add detail in a fixed place: where and when usually come after the object. `I learn English at home every day.`
- Adjectives come before the noun: `a small house`.
- English needs a subject: `It is cold`, `There is a problem`.

## Guided exercise

1. Take each of these and mark S, V, O:
   - `She reads books.` `We eat dinner.` `They watch movies.`
2. Build 10 sentences with this table (change one box each time):

   | Subject | Verb | Object | Place / time |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | I | drink | coffee | in the morning |
   | My brother | plays | football | on Fridays |

3. Reorder scrambled words into a sentence: `English / I / every day / study` -> `I study English every day.`
4. Expand one sentence step by step: `I read.` -> `I read a book.` -> `I read a book at night.` -> `I read an interesting book at night.`
5. Write 5 sentences about your family and read them aloud.

## What good looks like

- Your sentences have a subject and a verb every time.
- You put time and place after the object without thinking.

## Common mistakes for Hebrew speakers

- Dropping the subject or using the verb first: `Went to the shop.` should be `I went to the shop.`
- Placing the adjective after the noun: `a house big` should be `a big house`.
- Putting the time before the verb out of habit.
