# Step 12 — Negation

Stage C. Goal: tell the difference between `don't`, `doesn't`, `didn't` and `isn't`.

## Learn

| Situation | Negative | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present Simple (I/you/we/they) | don't + verb | `I don't like tea.` |
| Present Simple (he/she/it) | doesn't + verb | `She doesn't work here.` |
| Past Simple | didn't + verb | `They didn't call.` |
| `to be` | isn't / aren't / am not | `He isn't ready.` |
| Present Continuous | am/is/are + not + verb-ing | `I'm not sleeping.` |

- After `don't / doesn't / didn't` the verb is in the **base form**: `She doesn't work`, not `She doesn't works`.
- Do not use two negatives: `I don't know nothing` -> `I don't know anything`.

## Guided exercise

1. Make these negative: `He plays football.` `We saw the film.` `She is tired.` `I am working.`
2. Choose the right word: `He ___ (don't / doesn't) like fish.` `We ___ (didn't / don't) go yesterday.`
3. Correct: `She doesn't works.` `I didn't saw him.` `He don't know.`
4. Contradict 6 statements: `You live in Haifa.` -> `No, I don't live in Haifa. I live in Tel Aviv.`
5. Write 6 true sentences about yourself: 3 with `don't/doesn't`, 3 with `didn't`.

## What good looks like

- You choose the helper from the subject and tense in under a second.
- You do not double the tense marking.

## Common mistakes for Hebrew speakers

- Placing `not` like in Hebrew (`I not like`). Use the auxiliary: `I don't like`.
- `doesn't works`, `didn't went` (extra tense marks).
- Double negatives: `nobody doesn't know`.
- Mixing `isn't` with action verbs: `He isn't work` -> `He doesn't work` or `He isn't working`.

## Daily summary prompts

Write 6 negative sentences, two each for present, past and `to be`.

## Review questions

1. Which negative goes with `she` in the present?
2. What form is the verb in after `didn't`?
3. Why is `I don't know nothing` wrong?
