# Step 20 — Progress Test

Stage D. Goal: measure progress and plan the next week from your weak points.

## What the test contains

| Part | Task | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speaking | Record yourself talking for 3 minutes (topic: your week, a plan, or an explanation) | 3 min |
| Reading | Read a new text at your level you have not seen before | 5 min |
| Writing | Summarize that text in English (5-8 sentences) | 10 min |
| Compare | Compare with your baseline from step 1 and your earlier recordings | 10 min |

## Guided steps

1. **Record** 3 minutes without notes. Use past, present and future at least once, and two linking words.
2. **Read** a new short text. Underline unknown words (count them per 100 words, like in step 1).
3. **Summarize** it in English without copying sentences.
4. **Listen to your recording** and note: pauses, repeated mistakes, words you avoided, pronunciation points. Compare it with the step 1 recording.
5. Fill the scorecard:

```markdown
# Progress test (YYYY-MM-DD)
Speaking: fluency /5   accuracy /5   pronunciation /5
Reading: unknown words per 100 = ...  (baseline: ...)
Writing: mistakes per 5 sentences = ... (baseline: ...)
Listening: understood ~ ...%
Top 3 weak points: 1) ... 2) ... 3) ...
```

6. **Plan next week**: for each weak point choose a step to repeat (for example weak questions -> step 11; weak pronunciation -> step 7; vocabulary gaps -> steps 2 and 8) and set a daily 40-minute plan using the routine below.

## Daily routine (from the guide)

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 10 minutes | Review words |
| 10 minutes | Grammar and sentences |
| 10 minutes | Listening |
| 10 minutes | Speaking or writing |

## What good looks like

- You can show a measured change from the baseline (fewer unknown words, fewer mistakes, longer speech).
- Your next week targets the weakest areas, not your favourite ones.

## Common mistakes for Hebrew speakers

- Comparing yourself with native speakers instead of with your earlier self.
- Testing only the skills you are comfortable with.
- Making a plan that is too big. Four 10-minute blocks, done daily, beat one long weekly session.

## Daily summary prompts

Write the scorecard, the three weak points, and next week's plan.

## Review questions

1. What four parts does the progress test include?
2. How do you decide what to study next week?
3. What do you compare your results against?
