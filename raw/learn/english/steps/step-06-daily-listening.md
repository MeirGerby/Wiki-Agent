# Step 6 — Daily Listening

Stage B (Understanding English). Goal: listen to simple English for 5-10 minutes every day and understand the general idea before translating.

## Learn

- **Gist first**: the goal of the first listen is the main idea (who, where, what is happening), not every word.
- **Level**: choose audio where you understand about 70-80%. Slower speech for beginners (learner podcasts, graded videos, news for learners).
- **Repeat**: listening to the same short clip 3 times beats three different clips once.
- Do not translate each word while it plays; you lose the next sentence.

## Guided exercise (10 minutes)

1. Pick a 2-3 minute clip.
2. **Listen 1** (no pause, no subtitles): write one sentence in English about the main idea.
3. **Listen 2**: write 3 details (names, numbers, places).
4. **Listen 3**: write 3 new words or phrases you heard and guess their meaning from context.
5. Check with the transcript or subtitles. Mark what you missed and why (speed, unknown word, accent, linked sounds).
6. Log it:

```markdown
Date: ...  Clip: ...  Length: 2:30
Gist: ...
New phrases: ...
Hard because: speed / vocabulary / accent / linking
```

## What good looks like

- You can retell the main idea in one or two sentences.
- You can say why you missed something.

## Common mistakes for Hebrew speakers

- Stopping after every sentence to translate to Hebrew.
- Choosing material far above your level and understanding nothing.
- Only reading the subtitles instead of listening.

## Daily summary prompts

Write the gist in two English sentences, and list one new phrase.

## Review questions

1. Why listen for the gist first?
2. How many words should you understand for a good practice clip?
3. Why listen to the same clip more than once?
