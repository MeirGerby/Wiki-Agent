# Professional English

## Definition
Learning the vocabulary of your own field (Linux, programming, cyber) and explaining technical work in plain English.

## Mental Model
Learn terms in context from real documentation: man pages, README files, error messages and this wiki. Many terms are both nouns and verbs (`a commit` / `to commit`).

## Example
- `A firewall blocks traffic that is not allowed.`
- `This command shows the ports that are listening and the programs that use them.` (`ss -tulpn`)
- `Permission denied` -> `The user is not allowed to open this file.`
- Report: `I checked the failed logins. One IP address tried 12 times. I blocked it and changed the password.`

## Guided Practice
- Choose 10 terms from your current study (permission, process, service, firewall, log, packet, port, key, hash, evidence); write meaning, sentence and pronunciation; explain 2 commands in plain English; give a 1-minute explanation of a concept.

## Common Mistakes for Hebrew Speakers
- Mixing Hebrew and English in one sentence; wrong prepositions (`connect to`, `log in to`, `depend on`); treating `data` as plural in tech speech.
