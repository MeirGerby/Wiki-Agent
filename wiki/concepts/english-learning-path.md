# English Learning Path

## Definition
A 20-step guide from basic English to holding a conversation, understanding videos and reading texts, in four stages: A foundations (1-5), B understanding (6-10), C active learning (11-15), D intermediate and above (16-20).

## Mental Model
Study a little every day, use the material actively, and review at spaced intervals. Do not settle for reading explanations. The daily routine is four 10-minute blocks.

## Guided Practice
Daily routine: 10 min review words, 10 min grammar and sentences, 10 min listening, 10 min speaking or writing. Each step has a guided lesson file under `raw/learn/english/steps/`.
