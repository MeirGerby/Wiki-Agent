# English Daily Study Routine

## Definition
A 40-minute daily routine: 10 minutes reviewing words, 10 grammar and sentences, 10 listening, 10 speaking or writing.

## Mental Model
Study a little every day, use the material actively, and review at spaced intervals. Reading explanations alone does not build the skill. Short repeated blocks beat one long weekly session.

## Example
| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 10 minutes | Review words |
| 10 minutes | Grammar and sentences |
| 10 minutes | Listening |
| 10 minutes | Speaking or writing |

Spacing: revisit new words after 1 day, 3 days and 1 week.
