# English Linking Words

## Definition
Connecting ideas with `because`, `therefore`, `although`, `however` and `unless`.

## Mental Model
Each word has a position and punctuation rule. `although` and `because` join two clauses; `however` and `therefore` start a new sentence, followed by a comma; `unless` means "if not".

## Example
- `I stayed home because I was tired.`
- `I was tired. Therefore, I stayed home.`
- `Although I was tired, I went out.`
- `I was tired. However, I went out.`
- `I'll go unless it rains.`
`because of` takes a noun: `because of the rain`.

## Guided Practice
- Join pairs of sentences with a given word; rewrite one sentence three ways; add at least two linking words to the daily writing.

## Common Mistakes for Hebrew Speakers
- Two connectors for one idea (`Although..., but...`); missing comma after `however`/`therefore`; `unless you don't go` should be `unless you go`.
