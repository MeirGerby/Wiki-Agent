# English Sentence Structure

## Definition
The basic English order: subject + verb + object, with place and time after the object and adjectives before the noun.

## Mental Model
English needs a subject and a verb in every sentence: `It is cold`, `There is a problem`. Add detail in fixed places: `I study English at home every day.`

## Example
`I learn English.` -> `I learn English at home every day.` -> `I learn interesting English at home every day.`

| Subject | Verb | Object | Place / time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | drink | coffee | in the morning |

## Guided Practice
- Mark S, V, O in sentences; reorder scrambled words; expand a sentence step by step; write 5 sentences about your family and read them aloud.

## Common Mistakes for Hebrew Speakers
- Dropping the subject (`Went to the shop`); adjective after the noun (`a house big`); putting the time before the verb out of habit.
