# English Speaking Practice

## Definition
Building fluency by narrating your actions aloud (step 13), holding a 5-minute practical conversation (step 15) and thinking directly in English (step 19).

## Mental Model
Fluency before accuracy: keep going, fix later. If a word is missing, use a simpler one and note it for later. Direct thinking links an idea to English words without a Hebrew translation in between.

## Example
- Self-talk: `I'm making coffee. I'm opening the fridge.`
- Repair phrases: `Sorry, could you say that again?`, `Do you mean...?`, `Let me think...`
- Scenarios: introduction, ordering food, asking for help, work chat. Repeat the same one twice to improve.

## Guided Practice
- Narrate 3 minutes while cooking, replay it in Past Simple, then say a plan with `I'm going to...`. Record one session a week.
- Conversation: prepare 5 phrases and 3 questions, play both roles for 5 minutes, listen back, repeat.
- Thinking: label objects in English, think only in English for 5 minutes, write a to-do list with no Hebrew, retell a video in 3 sentences.

## Common Mistakes for Hebrew Speakers
- Translating in your head; whispering instead of speaking aloud; one-word answers; blunt requests (`Give me...`); waiting for the perfect word.
