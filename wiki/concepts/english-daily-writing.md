# English Daily Writing

## Definition
Writing 5 sentences a day about your day, checking mistakes and rewriting them correctly.

## Mental Model
Repeated mistakes are your real syllabus, so keep a mistake log. Read your text aloud once; the ear catches what the eye misses.

## Example
```markdown
Original: She go to work.
Fixed:    She goes to work.
Rule: -s with he/she/it
```
Checklist per sentence: subject? verb? right tense? singular/plural? `-s`? articles? prepositions?

## Guided Practice
- Write 5 sentences with one new word and one new phrase, correct each (keep the original), log the mistake type, and count types weekly to pick next week's focus.

## Common Mistakes for Hebrew Speakers
- Missing articles (Hebrew has only a definite article); wrong prepositions (`depend of`, `married with`); missing plural `-s`; long sentences copied from Hebrew structure.
