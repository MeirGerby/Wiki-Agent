# English Graded Reading

## Definition
Reading short texts at your level and marking words that keep coming back.

## Mental Model
Choose texts where you understand about 90-95% of the words. Extensive reading (lots of easy text) builds flow; intensive reading (a short text in detail) builds accuracy. A word seen 3 or more times is worth learning.

## Example
```markdown
Text: ... (word count)
Main idea: ...
Repeated words: ...
New words (with the sentence): ...
```

## Guided Practice
- Three reads of a 150-300 word text: main idea without a dictionary; underline unknown words and circle repeated ones; read aloud once. Answer Who? What? Where? Why? in English and add 3-5 words to your list.

## Common Mistakes for Hebrew Speakers
- Looking up every word; translating whole sentences into Hebrew; texts that are too hard; ignoring prepositions (`depend on`, `interested in`, `good at`).
