# English Listening

## Definition
Daily listening practice (step 6) and watching short clips with English subtitles in three passes (step 10).

## Mental Model
Listen for the gist first: who, where, what is happening. Choose audio where you understand about 70-80%. Repeating one clip three times beats three different clips once.

## Example
Three-pass method: pass 1 without subtitles (3 things understood); pass 2 with English subtitles (5 missed phrases); pass 3 without subtitles (note how much more you catch).

```markdown
Clip: ...  Length: 2:30
Gist: ...
Missed because: speed / vocabulary / accent / linking
```

## Guided Practice
- 10 min: listen 3 times to a 2-3 minute clip: gist, then details (names, numbers, places), then 3 new phrases. Check with the transcript.
- Linked sounds: `want to` -> `wanna`, `going to` -> `gonna`, `did you` -> `didja`.

## Common Mistakes for Hebrew Speakers
- Translating after every sentence; material too hard; reading only the subtitles; Hebrew subtitles instead of English.
