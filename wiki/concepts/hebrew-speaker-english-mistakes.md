# Common English Mistakes for Hebrew Speakers

## Definition
The recurring errors Hebrew speakers make in English, collected from all 20 steps of the learning path.

## Mental Model
Most come from carrying Hebrew structure over: no present-tense "to be", no indefinite article, different word order, and word-for-word translation. Knowing the pattern lets you catch it. This list is general knowledge added by the wiki maintainer, not part of the original guide.

## Example
| Hebrew habit | Wrong | Right |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No present "to be" | `I happy` | `I am happy` |
| Adjective after noun | `a house big` | `a big house` |
| Question by intonation | `You live here?` | `Do you live here?` |
| Double past marking | `Did you went?` | `Did you go?` |
| Negative without auxiliary | `I not like` | `I don't like` |
| Age with "have" | `I have 28 years` | `I am 28` |
| Missing `-s` | `She work` | `She works` |
| Wrong preposition | `depend of` | `depend on` |
| Present for ongoing | `I live here 5 years` | `I have lived here for 5 years` |
| Blunt request | `Give me coffee` | `Can I have a coffee, please?` |
