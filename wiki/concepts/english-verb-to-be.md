# The Verb To Be

## Definition
`am`, `is`, `are` in positive sentences, negatives and questions, plus `there is` / `there are`.

## Mental Model
English always needs the verb. Hebrew has no present-tense "to be", so learners say `I happy` or `She teacher`.

## Example
| Subject | Positive | Negative | Question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | I am | I am not | Am I...? |
| he / she / it | is | isn't | Is she...? |
| you / we / they | are | aren't | Are they...? |

Short answers: `Are you a student? Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.`

## Guided Practice
- Fill gaps, turn sentences into negatives and questions, answer with short forms, describe yourself in 5 sentences, and describe a room with `There is / There are`.

## Common Mistakes for Hebrew Speakers
- Leaving out the verb; using `are` with `he`/`she`; answering only `Yes` or `No`.
